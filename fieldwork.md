Shop: NIT Xerox & Printing Center

Location: Near Mega Mess, NIT Jamshedpur

Shopkeeper: Mr. Ramesh Mahato

Business: Photocopying, black-and-white printing, colour printing, scanning and spiral binding.

Things the Shopkeeper Said-

1.Unpredictable customer traffic: Customer visits are not consistent throughout the day. The shop sometimes experiences sudden rushes, particularly when students have assignment submissions, practical files or examination-related work.

2.Large printing orders create delays: Some customers request a large number of printed pages at once, which keeps the printer occupied for a longer duration and increases waiting time for other customers.

3.Difficulty managing peak hours: During busy periods, multiple students arrive together, making it difficult to manage orders efficiently. The shopkeeper generally relies on personal experience to anticipate busy periods.

One Thing That Surprised Me-
I initially assumed that the number of customers would be the main factor determining how busy a photocopy shop gets. However, I realized that the number of pages requested by each customer can be equally important.

For example, one customer requesting 100 pages may occupy the printer longer than several customers requesting just 5–10 pages each.

This made me realize that simply counting customers would not be sufficient for predicting workload. Our solution should also consider printing volume.
